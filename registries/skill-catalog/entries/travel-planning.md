# travel-planning

## Summary
Turns open travel ideas into feasible, decision-ready plans: structure,
research, logistics, operational recommendations. No booking intent required.

## When to use / vs alternatives
Trip planning, feasibility, itineraries, entry/transit/ground-transport
questions. Route to `booking` when the plan is concrete enough for live
availability or prices. Skip for stable travel facts or flight status
(`flightaware`).
